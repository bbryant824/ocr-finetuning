# EXP-015 — Full random in-context OCR comparison

**Completed and independently audited 4 October 2026.** All 1,722 full predictions saved; 24 fresh readiness outputs; zero fits, failures or retries. Both L4 apps stopped. [Reader findings and figure](../results/EXP-015.md). [EXP-014](EXP-014.md) remains the separate development/testing experiment.

## Purpose and frozen method

Compare the original Qwen model with eight/24 crop-transcript demonstrations against its matched zero-shot benchmark and the saved EXP-013 random LoRA checkpoints. Primary matched-bank control is 24 pages; 48/72-page results are larger-label-budget context.

[Recipe](recipes/read2016-known-box-icl.json) is unchanged from verified development. Source run `781d319efaa94b68bdd8027a2ae4e01c`, manifest SHA-256 `ac0a02a218a2142c6ee92ae964e7828ddfc65545dd6ca12f695815bb4493bdd6`; READ2016 v1.2.0 350 TRAIN/50 official VAL pages, with fixed diagnostic VAL8 and full VAL42/861 lines. Pinned Qwen/Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct revision `ebb281ec70b05090aa6165b016eac8ec08e71b17`, clamped eight-pixel padding, longest edge 1024 preserving aspect ratio, BF16, original transcription instruction, greedy maximum 64 new tokens and repetition penalty 1.1. Known boxes/reading order are supplied, not predicted. The training/selection fields are retained for checking the historical fit; ICL does not fit or run acquisition rounds.

Bank is the exact EXP-013 seed-824 random first selection, **24 TRAIN pages / 589 labelled lines**. Uniform per-query 24-line sampling without replacement uses a stable hash of seed 824, bank identity and query ID. Eight-shot is the first eight of that draw; all pairs retain their crop and original transcript. Query labels stay in the local oracle/evaluator. Every query is a fresh conversation; original weights stay unchanged. No retrieval, new seeds, prompt tuning or faithfulness audit.

L4 only, two CPUs and 32,768 MiB host allocation. One original-model worker at a time, 64-query resumable chunks, durable remote commits every eight returned rows. All 8-shot chunks complete and local coverage/metrics pass before 24-shot begins. Both passes use the same validation/query IDs and nested contexts.

## Reproduction

From the canonical checkout:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python -m active_ocr.region_pipeline \
  .local/verification/active-learning-source/runs \
  .local/verification/exp-015/icl-exp015-random-seed824 \
  --config experiments/recipes/read2016-known-box-icl.json \
  --strategy random --seed 824 \
  --baseline-from .local/verification/region-ocr-v2/region-exp012-random-seed824 \
  --fit-from .local/verification/exp-013/region-exp013-v2-random-seed824 \
  --icl-stage evaluate
PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python .local/verification/exp-015/audit.py full
```

Existing matching full outputs replay from immutable caches; no new inference is required to recompute results. For an independently approved fresh rerun, use a new namespace, `prepare` then `check`, and only then `evaluate`; preserve this completed evidence. Code/config changes require a new identity and relevant small check. The audit is local-only and never launches a model.

## Limits and conclusion

One fixed bank/context seed, one recipe, previously examined official validation, unknown writer grouping/pretraining overlap and no independent final test. EXP-013's historical adapter was constructed before Trainer seeding; it is a fixed realized checkpoint, not an initialization-matched average. Equal available pages do not establish identical transcript exposure, human annotation time or end-to-end cost. Both ICL conditions remain far above the saved matched-bank fine-tuned error rates. This pilot supports modest random-ICL improvement under its recipe, with mixed effect of more shots, not a general claim about every ICL approach or detection.
