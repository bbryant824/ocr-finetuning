The frozen plan was zero-label base-model prediction, then three random acquisitions of 64 TRAIN pages each (192-page budget), reset-fitting cumulatively. The same 16 VAL pages, `Seite0355`–`Seite0370`, were held fixed. The base prediction ran **before any training labels were revealed**. The first 64 selected TRAIN pages were fitted for 3 epochs/48 optimizer updates; all selected targets passed the upstream token preflight and the LoRA checkpoint passed reload checks. No validation truth entered the fit or selection requests. The run was deliberately stopped after the first acquisition because OCR quality failed its gate; it is **incomplete**, not a three-round result.

The raw receipts show repeated `regions` keys, page-number loops and out-of-range coordinates both before and after fitting. This is a model/recipe quality failure, not a metric parser repair opportunity. The first fit's 48 losses remained around 1.5 (first five 1.52–1.74, last five 1.26–1.61); 144 LoRA B tensors changed. Base prediction took 668.0 seconds and the trained prediction 1329.1 seconds. More rounds with this unchanged recipe were not justified. No useful OCR or active-learning gain is claimed.
