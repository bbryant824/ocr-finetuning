# EXP-011 — Qwen page-selection strategies against random

**Status: running; seed-824 k-center is complete, uncertainty methods and repeat seeds are pending.** This experiment asks whether choosing READ2016 TRAIN pages using the current Qwen model improves whole-page transcription more than the existing random control at the same annotation budgets. The control is [EXP-010](EXP-010.md). The measured outcome is validation CER/WER after fitting on 128 and 192 cumulative pages, not acquisition score itself.

## Fixed comparison

- Frozen READ2016 v1.2.0 source `781d319efaa94b68bdd8027a2ae4e01c`, manifest SHA-256 `ac0a02a218a2142c6ee92ae964e7828ddfc65545dd6ca12f695815bb4493bdd6`: 350 official TRAIN, 50 official VAL, document grouping unknown. No TEST set or pretraining-overlap audit.
- Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct revision `ebb281ec70b05090aa6165b016eac8ec08e71b17`. The first 64 randomly acquired TRAIN pages and their verified LoRA adapter are reused from EXP-010 (adapter SHA-256 `dcadcafbb1377807117ce04aa4a36fc58ad8d5e9039543618f5c9f01f5fe7cd3`). The initial selected-ID SHA-256 is `4dcaa13554ade40244da2c14c82271bf96d371c005e52a93cd730120cd68120f`.
- The [frozen base recipe](recipes/read2016-page-text.json) fixes image processing, prompts, greedy generation, 1,536-token cap, LoRA hyperparameters, three epochs, and page-text evaluator. Each subsequent fit resets from the same original base and trains on cumulative selected labels. VAL8 checks the run; the remaining VAL42 assesses the saved 128- and 192-page adapters. No VAL transcript enters selection or training.
- `entropy`: select the 64 remaining TRAIN pages with highest mean next-token entropy, normalized by log vocabulary size. `least_confidence`: select the 64 with lowest geometric mean probability of generated tokens. Both use the current adapter's **raw, pre-penalty logits** along its generated path, include EOS if emitted, score the emitted prefix if truncated, and break ties by page ID. Scores contain no reference transcript. These are separate strategies; they can share the first 64-page scoring pass, then must use their own 128-page adapters.
- `kcenter_greedy`: select 64 pages farthest from the labelled set in frozen, normalized Qwen projected visual-feature space; update distances after each selection. This is a proposed diversity baseline, not a claim that these features optimally represent handwriting. Its full 350-page GPU feature extraction and deterministic TRAIN-only 64-page selections at 128 and 192 pages have been verified; VAL42 at both 128 and 192 pages is complete for seed 824.

Seed 824 is the first end-to-end pilot. Repeat-seed comparison is planned for seeds 825 and 826 with seed-matched random controls and independently fitted first-round adapters; do not treat seed 824 as a mean or make a strategy-superiority claim from it alone. The optional uncertainty–coverage hybrid in the planning brief is deferred. The primary comparisons are paired CER/WER differences against random at 128 and 192 pages, alongside failed/truncated pages and scoring/fit cost.

The k-center 128-page checkpoint (`b6cbd43d72bd7fa84455658e0edae191ea61c092852535127048bc6511bf2fd4`) had one truncated VAL42 page; its [fit](https://modal.com/apps/bowenbryant24/main/ap-82uyBvyQbmLgvCHr8501d0) and [VAL42](https://modal.com/apps/bowenbryant24/main/ap-HuDGjxeIdtrbMTDfnaesy7) completed. The 192-page checkpoint (`a63579281ecd5733f1e91dda48bad473ba0bacb1d0575a96c47eee35c45cb6b0`) passed VAL8 and [VAL42](https://modal.com/apps/bowenbryant24/main/ap-engzWRIg3pDvL9MElyj4w9), with zero failed/truncated VAL42 pages. Its 192-page VAL42 CER was 0.0118 lower than random, while WER differed by only 0.0015. K-center reduced the maximum frozen-visual distance among unlabelled pages to 0.1087 at 128 pages versus 0.2420 for random. This feature-space coverage did not consistently improve OCR: 128-page validation was worse than random, and the 192-page difference comes from one seed only.

All raw receipts remain in ignored `.local/verification/al-strategies/`.

Mean token entropy is a sequence-generation uncertainty proxy, not exact sequence entropy or a guarantee of OCR error. Confidence can miss confidently wrong text and omitted text. The diversity baseline follows the greedy core-set idea of [Sener and Savarese](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.00489). The existing 42-page evaluation slice is official validation, not a document-independent final test; equal page counts do not imply equal human annotation time.
