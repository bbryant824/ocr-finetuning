# EXP-011 — Qwen page-selection strategies against random

**Status: completed; random, k-center, entropy and least confidence have all completed three paired seeds at both budgets.** This experiment asks whether choosing READ2016 TRAIN pages using the current Qwen model improves whole-page transcription more than the existing random control at the same annotation budgets. The control is [EXP-010](EXP-010.md). The measured outcome is validation CER/WER after fitting on 128 and 192 cumulative pages, not acquisition score itself.

## Fixed comparison

- Frozen READ2016 v1.2.0 source `781d319efaa94b68bdd8027a2ae4e01c`, manifest SHA-256 `ac0a02a218a2142c6ee92ae964e7828ddfc65545dd6ca12f695815bb4493bdd6`: 350 official TRAIN, 50 official VAL, document grouping unknown. No TEST set or pretraining-overlap audit.
- Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct revision `ebb281ec70b05090aa6165b016eac8ec08e71b17`. The first 64 randomly acquired TRAIN pages and their verified LoRA adapter are reused from EXP-010 (adapter SHA-256 `dcadcafbb1377807117ce04aa4a36fc58ad8d5e9039543618f5c9f01f5fe7cd3`). The initial selected-ID SHA-256 is `4dcaa13554ade40244da2c14c82271bf96d371c005e52a93cd730120cd68120f`.
- The [frozen base recipe](recipes/read2016-page-text.json) fixes image processing, prompts, greedy generation, 1,536-token cap, LoRA hyperparameters, three epochs, and page-text evaluator. Each subsequent fit resets from the same original base and trains on cumulative selected labels. VAL8 checks the run; the remaining VAL42 assesses the saved 128- and 192-page adapters. No VAL transcript enters selection or training.
- `entropy`: select the 64 remaining TRAIN pages with highest mean next-token entropy, normalized by log vocabulary size. `least_confidence`: select the 64 with lowest geometric mean probability of generated tokens. Both use the current adapter's **raw, pre-penalty logits** along its generated path, include EOS if emitted, score the emitted prefix if truncated, and break ties by page ID. Scores contain no reference transcript. These are separate strategies; they can share the first 64-page scoring pass, then must use their own 128-page adapters.
- `kcenter_greedy`: select 64 pages farthest from the labelled set in frozen, normalized Qwen projected visual-feature space; update distances after each selection. This is a proposed diversity baseline, not a claim that these features optimally represent handwriting. Its full 350-page GPU feature extraction and deterministic TRAIN-only 64-page selections at 128 and 192 pages have been verified across all three seeds.

Seed 824 is the first end-to-end pilot. Seeds 825 and 826 have seed-matched random controls and independently fitted first-round adapters. All four strategies have complete VAL42 results across three paired seeds. Do not infer a strategy advantage from seed 824 alone. The optional uncertainty–coverage hybrid in the planning brief is deferred. The primary comparisons are paired CER/WER differences against random at 128 and 192 pages, alongside failed/truncated pages and scoring/fit cost.

Mean token entropy and length-normalized output probability adapt sequence-generation quality indicators from [Fomicheva et al.](https://aclanthology.org/2020.tacl-1.35/); their machine-translation evidence does not establish OCR active-learning value. Entropy along one generated path is not exact sequence entropy, and confidence can miss confidently wrong or omitted text. The diversity baseline adapts the greedy core-set idea of [Sener and Savarese](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.00489), originally tested on image classification. The existing 42-page evaluation slice is official validation, not a document-independent final test; equal page counts do not imply equal human annotation time.
