## Fixed method and reuse provenance

At each shared answer prefix, let `zP` be the clean-query raw logits and `zN` the noisy-query raw logits. The decoder uses `zC = zP + (zP - zN)` (alpha 1), applies the existing repetition processor once, and permits tokens within log(0.1) of the maximum positive processed logit. Both branches retain identical correct support pairs and separate native caches. Only normalized query-image patches receive noise; no validation transcript enters either branch.

The noise schedule is `beta = sigmoid(linspace(-6,6,1000)) * (0.005 - 0.00001) + 0.00001`. At step 500, the cumulative alpha is 0.7447987198829651; `noisy = sqrt(alpha_bar) * clean + sqrt(1-alpha_bar) * epsilon`. Each query's original per-key seed namespace is retained. Its actual noisy query and seeded epsilon are saved once and reused exactly across all shot counts and resumptions. Tiny platform Gaussian differences are therefore recorded rather than allowed to change the reference condition.
