# Research question

Owner: research; date and related task: <fill>; evidence status: <literature / hypothesis / measured>.

## Search and sources

Question/relevance; queries, sources, search/access dates, inclusion/exclusion and versions.
For each key source record title, authors, year, venue/review status, verified URL/DOI, read scope
(full text/sections/abstract only), problem/method, source-reported findings with page/table,
limitations and relevance to this FYP. Prefer primary papers/docs; label preprints and leads.

## Synthesis

Compare agreement/disagreement, methods, controls, strength of evidence, transfer limits and gap.
Separate source claims, local measured results and untested hypotheses. For local results link
actual run/config/output evidence and assess matched budgets/seeds, variance and confounds.

## Recommendation to Planning

Falsifiable question, feasible candidates and random/base-model controls, suggested metrics and
annotation budget, assumptions/risks, and the smallest informative next experiment. Planning owns
the final testable design. Do not silently change model, dataset, splits or final methodology.
