# EXP-013 — Which known-box pages should Qwen learn from?

**Running: one-seed pilot; trained results pending.** Compare random, k-center, entropy and least confidence at 24, 48 and 72 labelled READ2016 TRAIN pages. The model transcribes supplied line crops; it does not detect boxes.

Each strategy selects its own first 24 pages from the original model rather than inheriting a random warm start. For lower cost, acquisition inspects three fixed lines per candidate page. Selecting a page reveals all its source transcripts, simulating annotation; every round trains a new adapter from the same base model on all acquired lines. Evaluation reads all 861 lines on the same 42 official validation pages.

The original model's score is reused from EXP-012 after identity checks and local recomputation, **not rerun**: CER **0.803646**, WER **1.087103**. CER measures character edits per reference character; WER measures word edits per reference word; lower is better. Final tables will show every matched-budget checkpoint, changes from random, capped outputs and page-selection overlap.

This is one seed, a sampled-line page score, and official validation with unknown document grouping and possible pretraining overlap. It cannot establish repeatable annotation savings or independent test performance. [Technical method and artifact locations](../experiments/EXP-013.md). No newly trained EXP-013 models exist at this initial record.
