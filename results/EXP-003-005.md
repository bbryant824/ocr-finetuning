# EXP-003–005 — The engineering pipeline worked; joint OCR did not

**17–18 September 2026 UTC · READ2016 · Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct**

## Why we ran them

After the first failures, the question was whether the full path could select pages, reveal only their TRAIN labels, fit on Modal, reload a checkpoint, and score held-out validation images. EXP-003 used the corrected Qwen adapter path. EXP-004 and EXP-005 tested the LLaMA-Factory training path for joint line boxes and transcription. These small runs were engineering checks, not a comparison of active-learning strategies.

## What happened

| Attempt | Training and validation | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EXP-003 | Untouched-model baseline, then one random acquisition of **2 TRAIN pages**; the same **2 VAL pages** were scored before and after fitting. | One round completed. The adapter reloaded in a fresh process, resume made no extra model call, and exports agreed with the run. Both VAL outputs failed before and after fitting; **CER 1.0, WER 1.0** at both points. |
| EXP-004 | Fit **16 initial TRAIN pages** and evaluated **8 VAL pages**. Eight more TRAIN labels were revealed and a 24-page fit began. | The initial fit and checkpoint reload succeeded, but the later fit was cancelled before an accepted checkpoint or post-acquisition score. **No acquisition round completed.** Initial CER was **0.9988**, WER **1.0**, and line-box F1 **0**. There was no untouched-model baseline. |
| EXP-005 | Repeated the **16 initial + 8 random acquired** TRAIN-page plan; fitted cumulative **24 pages** and scored the same **8 VAL pages** after each fit. | The full engineering pilot completed, including both fresh-process reloads, label-isolation checks, completed resume and matching exports. CER was **0.9988 → 0.9995**, WER **1.0 → 1.0**, and line-box F1 **0 → 0**. There was no untouched-model baseline. |

**CER** and **WER** are character and word edits divided by reference text length; lower is better. **Line-box F1** summarizes matched detected versus true line boxes, where 0 means none matched. Invalid or truncated outputs were recorded as failures, not silently repaired. In EXP-005, only 2/8 initial and 1/8 final page outputs were valid. CER values only slightly below 1.0 did not represent usable recognition.

## What we learned

EXP-003 proved a complete two-page remote round was technically possible. EXP-005 proved the LLaMA-Factory pilot could train, reload, evaluate and preserve a complete acquired round. **A working pipeline did not mean a working OCR model:** the final outputs still failed recognition and localization quality checks. EXP-004 remains an incomplete attempt, even though its initial checkpoint was valid.

All runs used original READ2016 labels as simulated annotation and the official TRAIN/VAL split; document grouping was unknown and no final test was used. EXP-003's two-page validation and EXP-004/005's eight-page validation are separate checks, not one pooled score. Random selection was the only acquisition method, so these runs provide no evidence of active-learning advantage or reduced human annotation time.
