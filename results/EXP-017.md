# EXP-017 — Does OCR ICL transfer to LightOnOCR?

**Development and checks in progress, 5 October 2026.** No full result is claimed yet.

EXP-015 showed modest Qwen improvement from random labelled crop examples; EXP-016 found that
transcripts alone retained much of that gain, while wrong crop/transcript associations harmed OCR.
This replication uses the same bank and validation queries with the original LightOnOCR-2-1B-base
from EXP-009. It tests whether those observations transfer to an OCR-specialized model.

Seven conditions: fresh zero-shot; correct paired, transcript-only and mismatched demonstrations,
each at 8 and 24 examples. Supplied READ2016 boxes define the crops; this is recognition with known
locations. No training, new adapter, retrieval or extra seed is introduced. The same Qwen crop
instruction is deliberately retained; LightOnOCR was trained for prompt-free extraction, so this
is a fixed-protocol test, not a claim that the prompt is optimal for LightOnOCR.

Local and L4 readiness checks precede VAL8/182-line integrity pilot and all VAL42/861 lines.
The final report will retain capped output in its primary line CER/WER and also show the inherited
page metrics, explain the comparison with Qwen EXP-015/016, and distinguish evidence from inference.
