# EXP-011 — Which pages should Qwen learn from?

**All three page-selection strategies and matched random controls are complete across three paired seeds.** We compare ways to choose the next READ2016 TRAIN pages for Qwen3-VL-4B whole-page transcription. Every path starts from the same untouched base model and first 64 randomly selected pages. At each later round it selects 64 new pages, reveals only their true TRAIN transcripts to simulate annotation, then trains a fresh adapter from the same base model on all 128 or 192 selected pages. Training and evaluation settings are fixed.

K-center chooses pages that expand coverage in frozen Qwen visual-feature space. Entropy chooses pages with the highest mean next-token uncertainty along their generated text. Least confidence chooses pages whose generated text has the lowest length-normalized probability under the current adapter. Random selection is the matched control. The reported holdout checkpoints were evaluated on the same 42 official validation pages. **CER** is character edits per reference character and **WER** is word edits per reference word; lower is better. Outputs cut off by the fixed token cap count as empty predictions.

| Labelled TRAIN pages | Random CER / WER ↓ | K-center CER / WER ↓ | Entropy CER / WER ↓ | Least-confidence CER / WER ↓ | Truncated: random / k-center / entropy / confidence |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 0.7902 / 1.0019 | same checkpoint | same checkpoint | same checkpoint | 10 / 10 / 10 / 10 |
| 64 | 0.5025 / 0.8643 | same checkpoint | same checkpoint | same checkpoint | 3 / 3 / 3 / 3 |
| 128 | 0.2946 / 0.6841 | 0.3113 / 0.6976 | 0.2766 / 0.6770 | 0.2825 / 0.6781 | 1 / 1 / 0 / 0 |
| 192 | 0.2112 / 0.5589 | 0.1994 / 0.5574 | 0.1934 / 0.5409 | 0.1930 / 0.5473 | 0 / 0 / 1 / 0 |

**The single-seed result is mixed.** K-center was worse than random at 128 pages, then lowered CER by 0.0118 at 192 pages while WER barely changed. Entropy and least confidence lowered both CER and WER at both budgets; their 192-page CER improvements versus random were 0.0178 and 0.0182. The 128-page random run truncated one validation page while both uncertainty methods truncated none; at 192 pages entropy truncated one while random and least confidence truncated none. This seed alone did not establish a repeatable advantage. The three-seed comparisons are below. Uncertainty scoring also took much longer than frozen visual-feature extraction; observed spans are in the technical record.

Across three paired seeds, k-center’s change from random was:

| Labelled pages | Mean paired CER change ± SD | Mean paired WER change ± SD | Direction across seeds |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 128 | +0.0060 ± 0.0107 | +0.0145 ± 0.0021 | WER worse in all three |
| 192 | −0.0045 ± 0.0085 | +0.0004 ± 0.0100 | Mixed |

Negative changes favor k-center; positive changes favor random. At 128 pages, visual diversity did not improve OCR and consistently increased WER. At 192 pages, its small mean CER improvement came with mixed seed-level outcomes, while mean WER was effectively tied. These three runs support **no consistent k-center advantage** at the tested page budgets. Full per-seed CER/WER and failed-page counts are in the technical record and raw receipts.

Across three paired seeds, entropy's change from random was:

| Labelled pages | Mean paired CER change ± SD | Mean paired WER change ± SD | Reading |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 128 | −0.0103 ± 0.0069 | −0.0011 ± 0.0082 | CER lower in all three; WER mixed |
| 192 | +0.0008 ± 0.0218 | −0.0017 ± 0.0144 | No consistent advantage |

Negative changes favor entropy. At 192 pages, seed 826's entropy model cut off on two validation pages while its random control cut off on none; this accounts for the sign of that seed's CER gap in a post-hoc common-page check. The primary metric still counts all 42 pages and shows no reliable entropy benefit at 192 pages. Random also had a truncated page in two of the three 128-page runs, so the 128-page CER difference needs caution. The technical record contains the per-seed values and this diagnostic.

Across three paired seeds, least confidence's change from random was:

| Labelled pages | Mean paired CER change ± SD | Mean paired WER change ± SD | Reading |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 128 | +0.0134 ± 0.0385 | +0.0113 ± 0.0158 | Mixed; seed 825 had three capped pages |
| 192 | −0.0041 ± 0.0248 | −0.0007 ± 0.0131 | Mixed; seed 826 had two capped pages |

The original seed-824 confidence gains did not repeat consistently. A post-hoc check on pages uncapped in both paths reverses some paired CER signs, showing that fixed-length generation failures materially affect these small validation comparisons. The full 42-page scores remain primary. **None of the tested selectors shows a consistent advantage over random at both budgets**, and uncertainty scoring required substantially more GPU time than random selection or frozen visual-feature extraction.

These are **official validation** results, not a document-independent final TEST result. Document grouping and possible model-pretraining overlap are unknown. Equal page counts also do not measure human annotation time.
