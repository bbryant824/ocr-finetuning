# EXP-006–008 — Why the Qwen joint box-and-text recipe was paused

**19 September 2026 UTC · READ2016 · Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct + LLaMA-Factory**

## Why we ran them

The earlier pilot completed technically but could not read or locate lines reliably. These three sequential checks asked whether more labelled pages, stronger training settings, or a simpler output format would make **full-page line boxes and transcriptions** usable. Each check predicted with the untouched model before revealing TRAIN labels, then selected pages randomly and fitted a fresh adapter on the cumulative selected set.

## What happened

| Attempt and change | Fixed validation slice | Untouched model | After fitting | Stopping point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EXP-006: original nested-JSON recipe | 16 pages | 2 valid outputs; CER **0.9992** | 64 TRAIN pages: 1 valid output; CER **0.9997** | Stopped after round 1. |
| EXP-007: more image pixels, wider LoRA targets and stronger learning-rate schedule | 8 pages | 0 valid outputs; CER **1.0** | 64 TRAIN pages: 0 valid outputs; CER **1.0** | Stopped after round 1. |
| EXP-008: one compact `box|"text"` row per line instead of nested JSON | 8 pages | 0 valid outputs; CER **1.0** | 64 pages: 0 valid; 128 pages: 0 valid; CER **1.0** at both | Stopped after round 2. |

**CER** is character edits divided by reference characters; lower is better. **Valid outputs** are complete pages satisfying the required box/text format. Across all reported stages, **WER was 1.0 and matched-line box F1 was 0**: words were not recognized usefully and no reference line boxes matched. Truncated or invalid generations counted as failed outputs. The 16-page EXP-006 slice and eight-page EXP-007/008 slices are separate checks; their scores should not be pooled.

The fits completed and checkpoints reloaded, but raw generations repeated text or boxes, made unusable coordinates, or ran into the token limit. EXP-008 showed that removing nested JSON was insufficient: the model learned some row syntax yet still produced no complete valid page. The planned third 64-page acquisition was therefore not run under that recipe.

## What we learned

These were **failed OCR-quality gates**, not completed three-round active-learning results. The evidence justified pausing this particular Qwen full-page joint recipe; it does not show that every Qwen configuration or joint OCR approach is impossible. It motivated the separate recognition-only LightOnOCR control in [EXP-009](EXP-009.md), which does **not** detect line boxes.

The official READ2016 350 TRAIN / 50 VAL split was used for engineering verification, with unknown document grouping and no final test. Original labels simulated annotation; only selected TRAIN truth was available to fitting, and no human annotation time was measured. There is no model-informed selection comparison here.

The frozen recipes, run IDs, checkpoint and raw-output evidence, and private artifact locations remain in the technical records: [EXP-006](../experiments/EXP-006.md), [EXP-007](../experiments/EXP-007.md) and [EXP-008](../experiments/EXP-008.md).
